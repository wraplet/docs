- `dependent wraplet` - A wraplet that depends on other wraplets, typically through the `DependencyManager`.
- `simple wraplet` - A wraplet that doesn't depend on other wraplets or nodes.
- `pure wraplet` - A wraplet that interacts with his own node only.
- `impure wraplet` - A wraplet that directly interacts with nodes other than his own.
